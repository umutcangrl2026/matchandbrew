Data Deletion

This page explains how to delete your data in Match & Brew, a game developed by Umutcan Gürel. The game has no accounts; your game progress is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, also backed up to your Google account.

1. Deleting your game data

Level stars, Beans, boosters, cups and settings are stored on your device; there is no copy on our servers. To delete them, use these options:

  • Uninstall the App from your device.
  • Or go to Android Settings → Apps → Match & Brew → Storage → Clear data.
  • If Android backup is on, you can delete the device backup in your Google account from the Backups section of Google Drive.
  • If you use Google Play Games cloud save, you can remove that backup in your Google Play Games profile settings by deleting the saved game data.

2. Deleting your advertising ID

Ads (Google AdMob) use your device's advertising ID. To delete or reset it, go to Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" (or "Reset advertising ID"); previous ad data will then no longer be linked to your device.

In the European Union and the UK you can change your ad and analytics consent at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options.

3. Requesting deletion by email

To delete other data, email umutcan.grl2026@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request – Match & Brew":

  • If you made a purchase, include the order number from your Google Play receipt (it starts with GPA.); we will delete your anonymous record in our purchase validation service (RevenueCat). You can get "Remove Ads" back later with "Restore purchases" in the game.
  • We reply to your request within 30 days.

4. What is deleted and what is kept

Your data is deleted or kept as follows:

  • Game progress (on your device): deleted immediately when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Cloud save (Google Play Games): kept in your Google account until you delete it in your Play Games settings; we cannot access this backup.
  • Analytics events (Google Analytics for Firebase): linked to a random app instance ID that does not directly identify you; because this ID is never sent to us, individual records cannot be found and deleted. User- and event-level data is deleted automatically after at most 14 months; only anonymous aggregate reports remain.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): contain no personal data; deleted automatically after at most 90 days.
  • Ad data (Google AdMob): kept according to Google's privacy policy; once you delete your advertising ID it is no longer linked to new data.
  • Purchase records: your anonymous record at RevenueCat is deleted on request. Google Play's payment records are kept by Google for legal (tax, accounting) obligations.
